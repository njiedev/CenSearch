data-article-body="true"><div class="MarkdownContent_markdownBody__v5MYy markdown-body"><p>Don&#x27;t post other people&#x27;s personal information. This includes:</p>
<ul>
<li>Personal, private email addresses</li>
<li>Phone numbers</li>
<li>Physical addresses or other private location information</li>
<li>Bank account information or credit card numbers</li>
<li>Social Security/National Identity numbers</li>
<li>Passwords</li>
<li>Voter information</li>
<li>Medical information and personal biometric data</li>
<li>Other private information that may pose a safety or security risk</li>
</ul>
<p>We may consider other information, such as photos or videos that were taken or distributed without the subject&#x27;s consent, to be an invasion of privacy, especially when such material presents a safety risk to the subject, such as in the case of intimidation or harassment.</p>
<p>GitHub will take context into account as well as whether the reported content is publicly available elsewhere. Please note, however, that while sharing publicly available content may not be a violation of this policy, if the information is shared with the intent to harass or incite other abusive behavior, it may violate our prohibition against <a href="/en/site-policy/acceptable-use-policies/github-bullying-and-harassment">bullying and harassment</a>.</p>
<p>For more information, or to learn how to report a violation, see our <a href="/en/site-policy/content-removal-policies/github-private-information-removal-policy">Private Information Removal Policy</a> and our instructions for <a href="/en/communities/maintaining-your-safety-on-github/reporting-abuse-or-spam">Reporting Abuse</a>.</p></div></div></div></div></div></main><div
